WASHINGTON, D.C./October 14, 2026 – Today, Special Counsel Charles Baldis formally presented Dr. Brett Wyrick, Deputy Federal Air Surgeon, with the Office of Special Counsel’s 2026 Outstanding Public Servant Award. 

OSC [announced](https://www.osc.gov/news/2026-07-30/osc-recognizes-faa-whistleblower-with-outstanding-public-servant-award-for-protecting-public-safety-in-airspace-on-national-whistleblower-day/) the award on July 30, 2026, National Whistleblower Day. During the ceremony, Special Counsel Charles Baldis presented Dr. Wyrick with the award and praised his decision to raise safety concerns.

“Dr. Wyrick, it is a privilege to give you this award,” Baldis said. “You saw a risk to the people who fly, and to the people who keep them safe in the air, and you would not look past it.”

Baldis said that, as Deputy Federal Air Surgeon, Dr. Wyrick identified that medical officers in the FAA’s Office of Aerospace Medicine were too few for the work before them. He said certificates and clearances for pilots and air traffic controllers were falling behind, hundreds of airmen continued flying while flagged medical issues had not been fully reviewed, and some personnel were discouraged from reporting an illness, injury, or new medication because a report could ground them for months.

“New controllers, hired to ease shortages in the towers, were waiting on medical clearances that should not have taken so long,” Baldis said.

“Your disclosure moved the FAA to hire additional medical officers and to clear these cases with more care and more speed,” Baldis said. “Those changes made a real difference. That is what this award is for: not simply for the difficulty of speaking up, but the good that followed from it.”

Baldis also emphasized the broader public significance of Dr. Wyrick’s actions.“You put that warning on the record because safety in the national airspace is not an internal matter,” he said. “It belongs to everyone who boards a plane, and to the families waiting for them on the ground.”

“On behalf of the Office of Special Counsel, thank you,” Baldis said. “Dr. Wyrick, please accept the 2026 Outstanding Public Servant Award.”
